Leakling is an AI-powered improvement tool for Valorant: it watches your
own gameplay, detects the moment behind each death, and compares it to how
the same situation could play out flawlessly — then surfaces the exact
habit to fix. It is NOT a stat tracker.

WHAT TO LOOK AT
- In-game death overlay: kill-feed detection with OCR-confirmed nameplates;
  fires only on your real deaths.
- AI flaw analysis: on-device model classifies each death clip into
  actionable flaw types.
- Leak profile: per-death tagging builds a personal habit profile with a
  next-match focus.
- Privacy-first: everything runs locally. No cloud upload, no recording,
  data never leaves the device.
