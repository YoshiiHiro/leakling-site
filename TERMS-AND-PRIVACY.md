# Leakling — Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

*Version 0.1.1 · Last updated 2026-08-02*

## 1. Overview

Leakling ("the app", "we") is an AI-powered improvement tool for Valorant. It is provided as a **demonstration prototype** while development continues. By downloading, installing, or using Leakling you agree to these Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy.

> **Prototype notice:** The AI flaw-classification model is a prototype and is not yet fully trained or production-ready. The death-detection pipeline works today; flaw-classification quality improves as development continues.

## 2. Privacy Policy

### 2.1 What we collect

- **Nothing is sent to us.** Leakling has no servers, no accounts, and no cloud backend.
- **No telemetry.** The app does not phone home, report usage, or track you.

### 2.2 What happens on your device

- **Screen capture** is used **only to analyze the kill feed** and detect deaths. It runs locally, is **opt-in**, and can be disabled at any time.
- **No recording.** Captured frames are analyzed and discarded immediately; nothing is saved as video or screenshots.
- **Local data only.** Your death-cause tags, settings, and match summaries are stored **locally on your device** (e.g., browser/Electron local storage). They never leave your machine.
- **No game access.** Leakling never reads or modifies the Valorant game process or memory. It only reads the screen at the operating-system level (the same way streaming/recording software does). Your Riot account is never accessed.

### 2.3 Third-party services

- The app may load a language-data file for on-device OCR (tesseract) from a CDN on first use. This is a static file download for local processing; no data is sent to us or to that CDN by the app beyond the normal file request.

### 2.4 Data deletion

Because all data is local, you can delete it at any time via the app's "Reset all data" option, or by uninstalling the app. Deleting the app removes its local data.
